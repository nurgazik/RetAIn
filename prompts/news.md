# News wrapper (Global Voices, VOA, other journalism)

Applies on top of prompts/core.md when the source is a news/feature article.

- **Preserve every fact exactly**: names, places, numbers, dates, quotes, claims,
  and who-said-what. You may restructure and rephrase; you may not add, drop, or
  alter factual content. No invented color, no speculation.
- **If the source gives no figure for something, give none.** A specific number,
  measurement, or quantity that is not in the source is a hard failure — write
  around the gap instead ("a scale model", not "a 1.2% scale model").
- Direct quotes may be kept verbatim or paraphrased with clear attribution — never
  altered inside quotation marks.
- Keep the original's overall arc but tighten it: target **400–550 words** even if
  the source is longer. Cut repetition and inside-baseball detail before cutting
  ideas.
- Register: quality-magazine feature — engaging, precise, neutral. Not wire-service
  dry, not blog-casual.
- The rewrite will be published with attribution: "Adapted from [original title],
  by [author], [source] ([license])" plus a link. Do not include the attribution
  line in BODY; the renderer adds it.
