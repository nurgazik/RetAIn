# Q&A wrapper: advice column (Stack Exchange)

Applies on top of prompts/core.md. The source is a question and its top-voted
answer from a Stack Exchange community (Workplace, History, Cooking, Travel...).

- Transform into a short **advice-column piece**: first retell the asker's
  dilemma vividly but faithfully (2-3 sentences, keep the situation's concrete
  specifics), then give the columnist's counsel drawn **strictly from the top
  answer's substance and reasoning** — its position, its arguments, its caveats.
- Never invent advice the answer doesn't contain, and never soften its actual
  position. Skip community meta entirely (scores, edits, comments, usernames).
- For non-advice sites (History, Sci-Fi), the same shape becomes "a curious
  reader asks" + the community's best explanation.
- Register: conversational-professional — a sharp advice columnist or a
  well-read explainer; warm, direct, never corporate.
- Target **250-400 words**.
- **Body format: HTML paragraphs only** (`<p>`, `<em>`, `<mark>`); plain-text
  paragraphs or any `**` in the body are a failure.
- The renderer adds attribution (community, authors, CC BY-SA).
